The Story

Why 828 Construction Exists

828 Construction was founded by Joe P — a builder with over 20 years of hands-on experience in construction, building science, and project management across Southern California.

After two decades in the field, Joe saw a pattern: homeowners were consistently being underserved. Projects that should have been straightforward became drawn-out problems.

828 was built to change that. Every project is approached with the full weight of building science knowledge — understanding how materials perform, how structures behave under real conditions, and how to build things that last.

"We're not the biggest company in the South Bay. We're selective about the work we take on — because quality requires attention, and attention has limits."

— Joe P, Founder

That selectivity is intentional. Each project receives the same level of scrutiny — beginning with a full understanding of what the building actually needs, not just what looks good on a proposal.

Remediation work is done at the source. ADUs are permitted, scoped, and built to perform. Consulting gives clients clarity before they spend a dollar.
