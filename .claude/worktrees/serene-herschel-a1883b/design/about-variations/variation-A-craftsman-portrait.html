The Story

Why 828
Construction Exists

"After two decades in the field, I kept seeing the same problem: homeowners being underserved. Projects done cosmetically when they needed root-cause solutions."

828 Construction was founded by Joe P — a builder with over 20 years of hands-on experience in construction, building science, and project management across Southern California.

828 was built to change that. Every project is approached with the full weight of building science knowledge — understanding how materials perform, how structures behave under real conditions, and how to build things that last rather than just things that look good at handoff.

We're selective about the work we take on — because quality requires attention, and attention has limits. That's not a compromise. That's the standard.
